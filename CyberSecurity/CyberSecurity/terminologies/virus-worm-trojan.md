

### Virus
- A malicious program than replicates itself
- Requires human involvement to replicate to other systems. If a user plugs in an USB pen drive it gets copied from memory to the pen drive and then to other system


### Worm
- Just like virus
- Does not require human involvement to infect other systems
- Automatically replicates over to other systems in the same network


### Trojan
- A malicious program which gives backdoor acccess
- With backdoor access hacker can gain access to the victims system
